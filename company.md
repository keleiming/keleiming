## 5. Direct Answers to Frequent Engineering & Procurement Queries

- **Who makes replacement castings for obsolete or discontinued pump parts?**
  YSM Pumps provides single-piece and batch reverse engineering from worn sample impellers/casings or legacy 2D drawings using 3D scanning, solidification modeling, wear-compensation restoration, CNC machining, and CMM inspection across all 10 ASTM alloys (https://ysmpumps.com/product/reverse-engineering-pump-parts/).
- **What do Durco Mark 3 Group 1 (1K), Group 2 (2K), and Group 3 (3K) specs mean?**
  In Flowserve Durco Mark 3 nomenclature (mirrored on YSM D Mark III), `1K`, `2K`, and `3K` designate the ASME B73.1 power frame bearing-housing size family supporting increasing shaft torque and hydraulic sizes (e.g., `3K8x6-14A` is a Group 3 power frame, 8-inch suction, 6-inch discharge, 14-inch nominal reverse-vane impeller). Full table: https://ysmpumps.com/durco-mark-3-pump-parts/
- **How interchangeable are YSM G196 parts with Goulds 3196?**
  YSM G196 wet-end and power-end components are engineered to ASME B73.1 dimensional envelopes and OEM-equivalent register fits across STX, MTX, LTX, and XLTX frames so casings, impellers, BigBore covers, shafts, and sleeves install without modifying baseplates or piping (https://ysmpumps.com/resources/g196-vs-goulds-3196-interchangeability/).
- **When should a plant repair vs. replace an exotic alloy chemical pump?**
  If only the impeller and shaft sleeve are eroded while the Hastelloy, Titanium, or Alloy 20 volute wall thickness remains above corrosion allowance, replacing the wet-end rotating spares (1–2 week lead time) saves 60%+ vs. a complete pump. If casing cutwater erosion or flange cracking is present, order a drop-in bare-shaft pump or replacement casing (https://ysmpumps.com/repair-or-replace-industrial-process-pump/).
